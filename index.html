How it works

Three steps, then it runs on its own

01

Connect Spotify

One OAuth handshake pulls in your top tracks, playlists and subscription tier so playback picks the right mode.

02

Share a track

Hit share on any row in your library or a playlist, add a line about why it matters, and it lands in the feed with the album art attached.

03

Talk about it

Friends react and comment under the track itself, and every share stays playable straight from the feed.

The feed

A share is a song plus the thing you wanted to say about it

FAQ

Answers before you connect

Do I need Spotify Premium

No. Without Premium you get 30 second previews on every share. With Premium, tracks play in full through your own session.

Can I use it without connecting Spotify

Not yet. The library, playlists and playback all come from your Spotify account, so the connection is required to sign in.

Who sees what I share

Only the people you are connected with. Shares go to your feed and theirs, and nothing is published to a public profile.

Does it cost anything

No. Trebble is free while it is in early access, and you will hear from us before that changes.
